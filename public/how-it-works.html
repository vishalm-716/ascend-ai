how it works

The identity loop — a continuous spiral of growth

Ascend isn't a to-do list or a habit tracker. It's a living system that senses who you want to become, curates the next small step, and adapts based on how it went.

1

Sense — Who do you want to become?

Tell Ascend your aspiration. Pick a domain — career, health, mindset, creativity, relationships, finances, learning — or describe your own. Share your current habits, energy level, time available, and biggest constraint. Ascend needs honest answers, not motivational ones.

2

Model — Build your identity model

Ascend creates a living identity model from your inputs: who you are, who you want to be, your values, non-negotiables, readiness level, and current state. This model is visible and fully editable — it's not a black box. You see exactly what the AI reasons over.

3

Plan — Generate your gap map

Your aspiration is decomposed into small, concrete gap steps — the delta between who you are and who you want to be. You can add steps manually, edit them, or let AI regenerate the map. Each step is categorized (habit, skill, mindset, project) and tracks prerequisites.

4

Curate — One action per day

Each day, the AI curator generates exactly one next-best action. It considers your identity model, your readiness level, your recent feedback (what you completed, rejected, or asked to swap), and your gap map. Occasionally, it includes a diversity injection — an adjacent idea outside your usual pattern.

5

Deliver — Act on your daily thread

You receive a curated action with a clear title, description, and — most importantly — a "why" tied to your specific context. Then you respond: Mark done (Ascend learns what fits), Not useful (Ascend learns what doesn't), or Give me something different (Ascend tries again, instantly).

6

Reflect — Weekly check-in

Once a week, Ascend asks for a one-minute reflection: rate your week (1–5 stars), share your energy level, and optionally write a note. This isn't busywork — it updates your readiness level and feeds back into the identity model, so the next week's threads are smarter.

7

Adapt — The loop gets smarter

Every signal feeds back into the system. Rejection adjusts the curator's pattern. Low readiness triggers gentler actions. Completed steps close gaps. The identity model evolves. Each week builds on the last in a continuous upward spiral — one small, chosen action at a time.
