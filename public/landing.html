The identity loop

A continuous cycle of sensing, modeling, curating, and adapting.

1

Sense

Tell Ascend who you want to become. Pick a domain, describe your aspiration, share your current habits and energy.

2

Model

Ascend builds a living identity model — editable and transparent. You see exactly what the AI reasons over.

3

Curate

Each day, one next-best action is curated from your identity model, gap map, readiness, and recent feedback.

4

Reflect & Adapt

Your weekly check-in feeds back into the model. Low readiness? Gentle day. High readiness? Time to stretch. The loop gets smarter every week.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Ascend.

Ascend is an AI-powered personal growth companion that holds a living model of who you are and who you want to become, then curates one next-best action each day.

It works across any area of life — career, health, mindset, creativity, relationships, finances, or anything you choose. The system adapts to your energy, readiness, and recent feedback so every action feels right-sized, not overwhelming.

Ascend's curator analyzes your identity model — aspiration, current state, values, readiness — and your recent feedback to generate one personalized action per day.

It includes a built-in heuristic curator that works without any API key, so the app is fully functional from day one. You can optionally connect any OpenAI-compatible LLM (OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek, Ollama, LM Studio) for richer, more nuanced curation. Every curator call is logged so you can see exactly how the AI reasoned.

No. Ascend works out of the box with its built-in curator — no API key needed.

If you want to power daily curation with a real language model, you can connect any OpenAI-compatible API key through the Settings panel or environment variables. OpenAI, Groq, DeepSeek, Ollama, and LM Studio are all supported.

Yes. All your data — identity model, gap map, check-ins, curated actions, and API keys — is stored in a local SQLite database on your own machine.

There is no analytics, no tracking, no telemetry, and no third-party data sharing. Your growth journey stays completely private. API keys are stored in the same local database and masked in all responses.

Traditional habit trackers optimize for streaks and guilt. Ascend optimizes for human potential.

It holds a living identity model instead of a static checklist. It curates context-aware actions instead of fixed reminders. It adapts to your energy and readiness each day. And it treats rejection as signal, not failure — when something doesn't fit, the system learns and adjusts.

Yes. Ascend is completely domain-agnostic.

You define the area of life you want to grow in. Career, health, mindset, creativity, relationships, finances, learning — or something entirely unique to you. The identity model, gap map, and curator all adapt to your chosen domain.

Once a week, Ascend asks you to rate your week (1–5 stars), share your energy level, and optionally write a reflection note.

This feeds back into your identity model's readiness level and informs the curator's decisions. Low-energy weeks lead to gentler actions; high-energy weeks lead to stretch goals. The model adapts naturally over time.

Yes. Ascend is open-source and free to use.

The built-in curator requires no API key and no external services. If you choose to connect a language model for richer curation, you pay only for your own API usage with your chosen provider.
